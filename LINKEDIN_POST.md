Safe to Send · Ep 13 — An agent can ask. Only a human can say yes.

Approval flows look simple until you try to break one. I tried, against a tiny approval inbox for agent-initiated sends:

→ The agent tries to approve its own request: refused
→ The Greek manager tries to approve a Croatian send: refused
→ The same person approves twice to reach "dual approval": refused
→ A 142,800 send with one approval: refused until a second person signs
→ Proof withdrawn after approval, before the send: blocked

That last one is the case most flows miss. An approval is for the delivery as it was when someone looked at it, not as it is when it goes out. So everything is re-checked at execution.
